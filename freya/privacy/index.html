Freya: Air Defence — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Freya: Air Defence (“the game”) is an Android game published by Yarka Prod (“we”, “us”). Contact: jaroslav17112007@gmail.com.

In short

The game does not collect, transmit, share or sell personal data. It needs no account, shows no advertising, contains no analytics or tracking, and works without an internet connection: it does not even ask Android for network permission.

What the game keeps on your phone

  • Your progress (unlocked systems, campaign results, in-game credits) and your settings (language, graphics level, brightness, sound).
  • A small technical report about your phone and the game’s performance (device model, Android version, graphics driver name, frame timings, free memory, a battery temperature estimate, the last events of a battle and the last lines of the game engine’s own log). The game uses it to choose safe graphics settings and to recover after a crash. It contains no account names, no save contents and no Android or advertising identifiers.
  • All of this stays in the game’s private storage on your device. We have no access to it, and it is never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes it.
  • The “Device report” button in the game’s settings copies this report as text, only when you press it, and only to your clipboard; whether you send it to anyone is up to you.

Permissions

The game asks for one permission: vibration, for haptic feedback. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files, accounts or identifiers (including the advertising ID).

Third parties

The game contains no third-party advertising, analytics or social-media components. It is distributed through Google Play. Google processes information about installing and running apps (for example crash reports from devices whose owners agreed to share them) under its own privacy policy, policies.google.com/privacy. We see only aggregate statistics provided to us by Google Play Console.

Children

The game is intended for teenagers and adults and is not directed at children under 13. Because it collects no data, it collects none from children either.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. To remove what the game stored on your phone, uninstall it or clear its storage.

Changes

If a future version ever handles personal data, this policy and the Data safety section on Google Play will be updated before that version is released.
